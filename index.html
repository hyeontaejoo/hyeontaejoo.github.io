About

Designing robotic systems from mechanical structure to intelligent control.

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Mechanical Engineering at DGIST, with research interests spanning robotics, mechanical control, and mechatronics.

I am particularly interested in robotic systems where mechanical design, kinematic modeling, sensing, and feedback control must work together. My current research includes tendon-driven continuum robots and multimodal human-intention recognition for robotic manipulation.

Developed a multimodal intention-recognition and control system for a 6-DOF PiPER robotic arm using gaze, human motion, and visual information.
