About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Mechanical Engineering at Daegu Gyeongbuk Institute of Science and Technology (DGIST). My research interests focus on robotics, particularly robotic systems that combine mechanical design, kinematic modeling, sensing, control, and human-robot interaction. I am currently interested in continuum robots, wearable and assistive robotics, robot manipulation, humanoid systems, and intelligent robotic control.

RESEARCH & PROJECTS

Selected Projects

Selected research projects involving robotic mechanism design, modeling, sensing, control, and human-robot interaction.

Nov. 2025 – Aug. 2026

Two-Segment Tendon-Driven Continuum Robot

Designed and developed a two-section tendon-driven continuum robot with ten tendon actuators for independent bending and extension/retraction.

Developed mechanical structures including 3D-printed discs, TPU ligaments, backbone structures, tendon-routing components, and electronics housings.

Implemented kinematic modeling, workspace analysis, inverse kinematics, multi-servo actuation, and IMU-based closed-loop feedback control.

PCC · DLS IK · MATLAB · Python · IMU · RGB-D Vision · Mechanical Design
Research Project

Mar. 2026 – Dec. 2026

Multimodal Intention Recognition for Robot Teleoperation

Developed a multimodal intention-recognition and control system for a 6-DOF PiPER robotic manipulator using user gaze, human motion, and visual information.

Processed Meta Quest Pro tracking information and stereo-camera data to estimate user-intended targets and desired object-placement coordinates.

Implemented coordinate-based robot control for pick-and-place tasks and contributed to system integration and experimental development.

Meta Quest Pro · Gaze Tracking · Motion Tracking · Stereo Vision · ROS2 · PiPER
